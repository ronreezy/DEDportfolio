About me

I have been a gamer for as long as I can remember. I grew up with the original NES, spent hours playing Donkey Kong Country on the Super Nintendo, played GoldenEye 007 almost every day on the Nintendo 64, and followed that interest into the Xbox era. When my father was stationed in Washington after I finished high school, games led me to the 3D Arts and Animation program at Clover Park Technical College, where I earned a certificate in 2006.

I wanted a stronger foundation in how things are formed in the physical world, so I studied studio art at California State University, Sacramento, with a focus on sculpture. Learning to see and build form in clay and other materials strengthened the way I approached digital modeling. At that point in my life, however, I did not want to spend my days alone behind a computer building 3D models. It took me time to understand that the part of game design I enjoyed most was building the game itself: creating systems, rules, and experiences that motivate people.

My career took a detour when I moved to South Korea to teach. I fell in love with teaching, especially the challenge of motivating students. Instead of relying only on traditional rewards, I began designing classroom systems inspired by games. In 2015, while looking for a graduate program that could strengthen my teaching, I discovered that this kind of work had a name: Learning Design and Technology. I completed my master's degree at San Diego State University from 2016 to 2018 and applied what I learned by building two gamified learning systems, one inspired by my favorite game, Kingdom Hearts, and another by Pokémon.

In 2023, I returned to Clover Park as a Curriculum and Instructional Design Specialist. The role brings together what I learned through art, teaching, learning design, and game systems. I also resumed Digital Entertainment and Design coursework to continue what I began in 2006. This portfolio gathers the work and experience I believe demonstrate the outcomes for the associate degree. Some gaps remain, and I am ready to complete additional work where needed.
