Game Study: Blankos: Block Party

Blankos: Block Party is a multiplayer online game where players can buy/customize NFT characters and create, share, and play in custom user-generated levels and participate in various social activities.

Interaction model:

Blankos features a third-person perspective interaction model, allowing players to control their character from a viewpoint behind and slightly above the character. This interaction model makes it easy for players to navigate the 3D game world and interact with other players and objects in the environment.

Player interaction:

The game offers both cooperative and competitive player interactions. Players can work together in user-generated levels to collect “vibes”, or they can compete against each other in races or other challenges. Additionally, players can interact through social hubs, trading, and chatting with other players.

Reward structures:

Blankos has various reward structures, including in-game currency (called moola), experience points, and cosmetic items. Players can earn these rewards by completing levels, participating in events, or achieving specific goals. These rewards encourage players to engage with the game and its content.

Progress shown to the player:

The progress in the game is shown through the player's level, which increases as they gain experience points from completing levels and participating in events. Additionally, players can track their progress through the collection of cosmetic items and achievements that showcase their accomplishments.

Agency given to the player:

Players have a high degree of agency in Blankos, as they can create levels, customize their characters, and choose which activities to participate in. This freedom allows players to tailor their experience to their preferences and playstyle.

Core Mechanic:

The core mechanic in Blankos revolves around the player's ability to buy, upgrade and stylize their NFT characters or create, share, and play in custom user-generated levels. This mechanic encourages creativity, social interaction, and community-driven content.

Game's Dynamic:

The game's dynamic is centered around the user-generated content and social interactions. As players create and share new levels, the game's content constantly evolves, ensuring a fresh and unique experience every time they play.

Information structure:

Blankos features an open information structure. Players have access to all available information, such as objectives, rules, and level layouts. This transparency allows players to strategize and make informed decisions while playing.
