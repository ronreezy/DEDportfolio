108.1, 108.2 - Unity in Wisdom - Narrative Outline.docx

Log Line:

In a dwindling Learning Enclave, artisan Jalen embarks on a transformative quest up Jisan's mystical path, wrestling with personal doubts, only to discover that true wisdom lies within.

Four Key Points:

The Learning Enclave faces a decline in student engagement and whispers of opportunities elsewhere.

Jalen, intrigued by a challenge to ascend Jisan, grapples with the journey's commitment.

Through collaborative experiences and trials, Jalen grows alongside fellow artisans and discovers the importance of unity in education.

At the pinnacle of Jisan, Jalen learns that true wisdom is not a tangible reward, but the knowledge and experiences gained through the journey.
