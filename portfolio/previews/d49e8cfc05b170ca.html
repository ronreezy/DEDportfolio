Brainstorming:

A story that is set in a mystical futuristic land where teams of players try to get to the top of a mountain where they will reach a temple full of treasures. They will encounter challenges along the way, which they must overcome using a combination of solo and cooperative play. It will be a story driven game with the emphasis on figuring out puzzles as opposed to moving a character around doing moves.

Time limit 10 minutes. Players start by rolling the dice, everyone at the same time. Your color needs a 10 to get to the next level. When they get 10, they have to answer a Riddle. If correct, they can move up. If not, then they can try the Riddle again. Same for everyone. You win when everyone makes it up to the top.

After playtesting (in class):

After play testing with my victims, the arbitrary roll 10 was not effective because it was too hard to track. Instead, we made the game board with spaces and you have to move around and land on certain spaces and answer trivia.

Next, we added a chance for teams to work together when answering questions. We all agreed that the collaborative nature of the game made it more engaging because we didn't feel alone.

The next improvement is to add to the game board so that when you move around, it's not just one space that is important. Other spaces provide points or hints. The purpose of the game should be to collect knowledge as you are walking around.

Or instead of just having one piece to move, the player has multiple pieces that can move up. Each turn players role and decide how they want to move and each piece and how many squares. Similar to the Korean game Yut-no-ri.

After playtesting (at home):

So I play tested at my house with the lady and changed the rules a bit. Instead of board spaces, there was just one big spot where the players waited to go to the next level and I changed what the dice did. Instead of using the dice for movement, I made it so that If you roll a 6, you get a challenge question, if you roll a 2 to 5, you get hints, and if you roll a 1 you get nothing. It was a little too hard to roll the six at first though, so for the first level you can roll a 6, 5, 4, 3 to get the challenge question. For the next level you need to roll a 6,5, 4. Next level 6 or 5 and for the final challenge you need to roll a 6. It's quite easy to not get a one or two at the beginning, so that makes it feel great for the players to move up very quickly and gets them invested in the game. I also added cards for each team.
