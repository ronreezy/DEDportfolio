Art 192A-02 Senior Seminar in Studio Art Flohr

Proposal

I am excited for the Senior Seminar Art class this semester. I have been thinking about what I was going to do, and I finally arrived on an idea that I have spent a little time in the past brainstorming. I realized that I enjoy Art when I am creating things that I am passionate about. A lot of the Art I have worked on in the past have influences from music, movies and things that I have extreme interest in. For this semester, I want to work on this idea that I played around with in the past, of a futuristic sci fi fantasy world, that looks very similar to ancient China. I have a story in my head, that I want to illustrate in real life.

What I plan to do is to create 10 different mini-sets or dioramas of the different scenes from the story. The protagonist of the story moves through many different places, and I want to show each place in detail. There are many reasons for why I choose diorama as the method. I have always loved dioramas. Growing up, I loved doing them. I loved seeing other peoples. I also think they are a good way to show different methods I learned while studying Art at Sacramento State. The background can be painted to show skills developed in painting and drawing. The objects filling the foreground will show skills learned in sculpture and also installation (in a much smaller scale). Finally, I plan to take all the ‘sets’ and use them in a stop-motion filming of the story.

In terms of time management, I plan to devote most of my free time to this project. My goal is to create and pretty much finish one diorama a week. I decided to take a full course load, so time management will be key to reaching this goal. I know I will have to put at least 10 hours in each week, but I hope I have time to do more. Right now, I know I want to put 3 hours in for Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and Monday. Tuesday through Thursday have heavy workloads, so I don’t see much getting done on these days. I have a break on Wednesday from 11 to 3, so this is where I will get all my Pachyderm stuff up.

As far as where I will be working, I think it will be out of my house. I have access to a lot of materials here, as well as the internet and my car. I am really excited for this project, so I am sure that will drive me into creating something that I will love.
