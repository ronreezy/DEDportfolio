DED108 Screenwriting

“Seize the Day”

Written by

Ronald Lethcoe

INT. CAMPUS CAFETERIA - DAY

Mark (M 21), an art student sits at a long table. His jeans have splatters of paint. His table displays sketch pads and art books, each page filled with vibrant colors and bold strokes.

Amy (F 21), a focused math major, arrives with a heavy backpack full of textbooks. She looks distracted. She takes a seat across from Mark. She eyes Mark’s books.

MARK

Hey Amy! I have an idea for my final project. I want to combine art and math. Visuals inspired by mathematical sequences. You know those spiral patterns you showed me?

AMY

Mark, I admire your enthusiasm, but I got equations on the brain right now. Finals are around the corner and my thoughts feel as chaotic as one of your abstract pieces.

Mark chuckles.

Mark

Amy, consider this. We could create something revolutionary. A blend of our worlds. I need your mind to perfect the math part, though.

He gestures toward a sketch of shapes spiraling from a center point - a Fibonacci sequence in visual form.

Mark

This sketch uses the Fibonacci sequence. I want to capture its elegance, its precision. But I need your perspective. Could you help, once your exams finish?

Amy

Mark, I wish I could help. But look…

She points to her backpack.

Amy

After finals, I have to prepare for the next semester. I don’t think I can spare the time.

Mark nods and his face falls a little. However, his eyes hold determination.

Mark

Come on, Amy. This could be fun. Art and math intertwining? A masterpiece in the making!

Amy looks distressed.

Amy

Fun is a luxury, Mark. I’ve got my studies to worry about.

Mark

You gotta live a little, Amy. We only have one life. Seize the day, right?

Amy

Tell that to my parents. If I get a B, my universe will collapse in on itself.

Mark

I think a splash of creativity might even help your math. Plus, we could spend more time…

Mark moves closer to Amy.

Amy

Mark, you’re not helping. You’re just making me distracted. I’ve got to go.

Mark watches as Amy leaves.

INT. MATH CLASSROOM - DAY

Amy sits at a desk in her math class, her textbooks spread out before her. The professor (F 45) is at the front, chalk in hand. She explains Fibonacci sequences.

Amy tries to focus but looks distracted.

Amy

(To herself) Seize the day...

Her eyes wander around the classroom. She focuses on a banner hanging above the chalkboard. It reads, ‘Carpe Diem.’

PROFESSOR

Fibonacci was not afraid to take chances, you see. His sequence is not just numbers. It’s a leap of faith, an exploration of the unexpected…

Amy’s eyes drift to a fellow student (M 22) across the room. He wears a T-shirt with the acronym ‘YOLO’ emblazoned across it.

Professor

Fibonacci was an interesting fellow. He mingled with artists of his time, gaining inspiration. It is widely believed that his interaction with these creative minds influenced his work on the Fibonacci sequence…

Amy looks like she just came up with a great idea.

EXT. CAMPUS GROUNDS - DAY

The bell rings. Amy rushes out of the classroom. She looks for Mark. She goes to his homeroom. She goes to the cafeteria. He is nowhere to be found.

Amy looks like she just came up with a great idea.

EXT. CAMPUS GROUNDS - SECLUDED TREE - DAY

Mark sits under the tree. He sketches clouds in his sketchbook. Amy approaches.

Amy

Mark, I have news…

Mark appears cold, distant. He cuts her off.

Mark

Let me guess, more math problems?

Amy notices Mark’s hurt expression.

Amy

Mark, I didn’t mean to dismiss your work earlier…

Mark

It's not about dismissing, Amy. It's about understanding. You didn't understand, just like I don't understand why you suddenly want to be a part of my world.

Amy looks down, then meets Mark's gaze.

AMY

You're right, Mark. I didn't understand. But I want to. I want to help you with this project. And, well, maybe we can understand each other's worlds a bit better along the way.

Mark hesitates. Then he smirks.

Mark

Alright, but on one condition… we approach this with a ‘seize the day’ attitude.

Amy rolls her eyes. Mark’s grin widens.

Mark

Great! Now, we all know the best inspiration comes from milkshakes, McDonald’s milkshakes to be exact.

Amy’s eyes light up. Her excitement matches Mark’s. The camera pans up to reveal the McDonald’s sign in the distance, its famous arches frame the words, ‘Seize the Day.’ The familiar jingle, "I’m loving it," plays as the screen fades.

FADE OUT.
