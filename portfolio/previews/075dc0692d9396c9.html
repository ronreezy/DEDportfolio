Title:

The Mountain Temple Quest: Tabletop Edition

Objective:

The goal of the game is for all teams to reach the top of the Mountain Temple within the 30-minute time limit.

Setup:

Place the game board on the table.

Separate the cards into each team’s Hint and Challenge decks and everyone’s Event decks, and place them face down near the board.

Choose the number of players (1-4) and distribute team tokens accordingly:

4 players: Each player takes one token.

3 players: Each player takes one token, and one token is left unused.

2 players: Each player takes two tokens.

1 player: The player takes all four tokens.

Place the team tokens at the starting position on the board.

Set a timer for 30 minutes.

Gameplay:

Players take turns in clockwise order. On each turn, the active player follows these steps:

Roll the dice.

Take an action based on the dice roll:

If the player rolls a 1, their turn is over.

If the player rolls 2-5, they draw a Hint card for the current stage their team token is on. The Hint card provides information to help their team progress. Players can share this information with other players.

If the player rolls a 6, they draw a Challenge card. The Challenge card contains a question or task that the player must complete. If they succeed, they can move one of their team tokens to the next level (maximum 2 out of 4 tokens can progress per successful challenge). If they fail, their turn is over, and the Challenge card is returned to the bottom of the deck.

The player may also choose to play an Event card from their hand (if they have any). Event cards can introduce unexpected twists, provide advantages, or hinder other teams. Event cards are drawn whenever a player successfully completes a Challenge card question or task.

The player's turn ends, and the next player begins their turn.

Winning the Game:

The players win the game if all team tokens reach the top of the Mountain Temple within the 30-minute time limit. If the time runs out before all tokens have reached the top, the players lose the game.
