DED108 Screenwriting

“Unity in Wisdom”

Written by

Ronald Lethcoe

EXT. LEARNING ENCLAVE - DAY

The Learning Enclave bustles with activity. Artisans showcase their crafts. Students gather, eager to learn. In a corner, smoke rises from a blacksmith’s forge.

YOU (GENDER NEUTRAL, 36) clutch a parchment. Names fill its surface. Concern marks your face as you scan the enclave. You need to find the missing students. You navigate the enclave.

[GAMEPLAY: Player must walk around the enclave and learn the controls by talking with people and doing puzzles.]

Deeper in, ARI (F, 49) captures your attention. She shapes clay. A group of young students watches.

ARI

Remember, each movement is an extension of your soul. The clay feels your intent. It’s a dance between your hands and the earth.

FEN (M, 18) tries to emulate her, but his clay contorts.

FEN

Seems my soul’s a tad clumsy today!

Ari

Every dance has its missteps. Keep practicing.

MIKA (F, 25) approaches you.

MIKA

You seem lost. What troubles you?

You show the parchment to Mika.

YOU

I can’t find Ana, Rori, and Bo. They’re essential for the Ember Tome reading tonight.

Mika gestures towards the distant forest.

MIKA

Ah, those three! I spotted them heading that way earlier. They joined a caravan leaving the enclave with all their stuff.

You look into the distance. Worry lines appear on your face.

YOU

I wonder where all the students are going.

INT. LEARNING ENCLAVE TEMPLE - DUSK

Golden lanterns cast a soft glow and reveal ornate pillars and ancient tapestries that tell the enclave’s storied history. The heart of the temple holds a grand wooden table, atop which the revered Jisan map is spread out.

KRISSA (F, 42), a monk with wise eyes, gestures for silence. GENREY (M, 51), a tall monk with a gentle presence, stands beside her. RONAN (M, 37), the youngest monk, observes the crowd.

From the shadowy edge, SIMON, a sleek robotic figure, activates a holographic interface which displays the declining student numbers. The numbers blink in red, which show the sharp decrease.

SIMON

Our student numbers have been plummeting at an alarming rate. Immediate action is essential.

LIYA (F, 57)

This cannot continue! We’re losing our legacy.

RIN (F, 46)

It’s the outside world; they tempt our students with empty promises.

VORO (M, 32)

We need to modernize our methods. Our traditions won’t suffice anymore.

SOLAN (F, 28)

Modernize? And risk losing the essence of the Enclave? Never!

KRISSA

Calm down, all of you! Arguments won’t solve our problem.

GENREY

Krissa’s right. We need to act, not argue.

RONAN

Perhaps it’s time to venture beyond the Enclave. Seek new knowledge to enrich our teachings.

RIN

A journey? With our dwindling numbers?

VORO

Maybe it’s our way forward. An expedition to find and reclaim the lost souls.

KRISSA

An interesting proposal. We need to weigh the risks and benefits. For now, let’s come together, unite in our purpose, and find a way to save the Enclave.

GENREY

Krissa speaks the truth. Together, we are stronger.

The artisans nod in agreement.

KRISSA

The Jisan map reveals paths our ancestors walked. We’ve forgotten some, but now we must retrace their steps.

RONAN

These paths lead to knowledge, wisdom, and perhaps a solution to our problem.

GENREY

Our ancestors faced similar challenges. They found answers; so can we too.

Murmurs ripple through the artisans. Some nod in agreement, while others exchange skeptical glances.

SOLAN

We’ve never ventured this far. The world has changed. How do we know we’ll succeed?

KRISSA

We don’t. But the Enclave’s survival demands we try.

DARIK (M, 39) steps forward, his robe decorated with symbols of the sky.

DARIK

I lead the Seekers of Skylore. We honor the Cornerstones of College Instruction. Join us for a quest of innovation and exploration.

HANSELLE (F, 40) steps up next, her robe adorned with green and gold patterns.

HANSELLE

The Scholars of the Verdant Archive awaits those who value Evidence-Based Practices. Walk with us as we cultivate wisdom.

JAYNA (F, 47) advances, her robe embedded with luminescent threads that mimic circuits.

JAYNA

The Navigators of the Deep Code seek those intrigued by Education Technology. Let’s chart the digital seas together.

Lastly, DINO (M, 52) stands tall, his robe emblazoned with a flaming book.

DINO

The Ember Tome needs guardians. Champions of Open Education Practices, join us to light the way for all.

Artisans shift with excitement and gravitate towards the tribal leaders who resonate with their passions and beliefs.

KRISSA

Choose wisely, artisans. The path ahead is uncertain, but united in our tribes, we will find our way.

The hall hums with determination as artisans start to form groups, united in their purpose.

INT. YOUR WORKSHOP – NIGHT

Crafted items fill wooden shelves, each in their precise place. You sit on a stool, fingers brushing a finely crafted artifact. Your gaze moves to the Jisan map on the table. The intricate details of the map capture your attention.

The door creaks. Mika enters.

MIKA

The workshop feels like a sanctuary. Every item tells a story.

You

Our ancestors crafted with such precision. But times change.

MIKA

Change brings challenges. But also, opportunities. The enclave needs to adapt.

You

This was my grandfather’s. He believed in blending old crafts with new knowledge. Maybe that’s our path.

MIKA

Then let’s rediscover that path together. The Jisan map might hold clues.

You

A journey? With the enclave in such a state? And who has the time?

MIKA

To save the enclave, we might make the time and step outside.

You place the artifact next to the map with determination in their eyes.

You

Then let’s prepare. Our ancestors guide us through this map, and we must follow.

EXT. BASE CAMP AT JISAN’S FOOT - MORNING

Artisans are scattered across the camp. Each tribe distinguishes itself with unique colors and symbols on their yurts. Artisans chat. Tools clang. Occasional laughter fills the air. You try to find your place amidst the tribes and wander with a sense of determination.

[GAMEPLAY: Player must walk around the base camp and learn about the different tribes.]

As you walk by Darik, he beckons you over.

DARIK

Ah, artisan! Ever looked to the skies for answers? The Seekers of Skylore know their wonders well. We honor the Cornerstones of College Instruction, always pushing the boundaries of what we know. Quest with us, and together we’ll soar to new heights.

Hanselle observes from a distance and approaches with a warm smile.

HANSELLE

Artisan, the Scholars of the Verdant Archive are always eager for inquisitive minds like yours. Grounded in Evidence-Based Practices, we dig deep, seeking truths hidden within the roots of wisdom. Will you join our journey?

You nod and move on. You are drawn to the soft blue glow that emanates from Jayna’s robes.

JAYNA

Drawn to the light, artisan? We, the Navigators of the Deep Code, sail the vast digital seas of Education Technology. With every wave, a new discovery; with every tide, a new lesson. Together, we can chart unknown territories.

Dino observes your curiosity and steps forward with a twinkle in his eye.

DINO

Young craftsman! The Ember Tome burns bright and we seek guardians like you. As champions of Open Education Practices, we light the path for all. By our side, you’ll not only learn, but you’ll also guide others.

You take a deep breath. You ponder the leader’s words with a contemplative expression.

[GAMEPLAY: Player must choose a tribe.]

TRIBE LEADER

Great choice, artisan! We are happy you have joined our tribe. Let’s prepare. Our ascent up Jisan starts in the ‘morrow!

EXT. THE WHIMSICAL WOODS - MORNING

The woods stand as a realm of wonder. A river gleams between colossal mushrooms. Vibrant caps cast distinct shadows. Luminescent particles float in the air and the woods glow ethereally.

You stand by the river. Your eyes focus on the opposite bank. Trees there display glowing symbols, each a visual clue to a puzzle.

Mika approaches, her gaze following yours to the symbols.

MIKA

These symbols… they resemble the Enclave’s crafts, don’t they?

[GAMEPLAY: Player must drag and connect symbols on their screen in the correct order. They represent the evolution of crafts in the Enclave.]

YOU

We might need to arrange these symbols in their order of creation.

The Tribe Leader observes from a distance, his posture alert.

TRIBE LEADER

Choose wisely. The woods guard their secrets.

As you rearrange the symbols, a connection forms between pottery and weaving. Mushrooms in the river begin to glow.

MIKA

Look! The bridge… it’s forming!

[GAMEPLAY: As the player successfully connect symbols, parts of the mushroom bridge rise. Once all symbols connect correctly, the bridge completes.]

The Tribe Leader nods in approval.

TRIBE LEADER

The path becomes clear. Forward, we move!

EXT. THE BABBLING BROOK - DAY

The Babbling Brook is calm. A pristine stream flows, bordered by dense greenery. The water mirrors the sky and trees and paints a serene reflection. Sunlight dances and creates patterns on the water’s surface.

You stand at the water’s edge, captivated by the shimmering surface. Beneath the clear waters, you spot stones with engraved symbols, each distinct and unique.

Mika joins you and points to a series of similar symbols etched onto nearby trees.

MIKA

Look at the trees and the stones. There’s a connection.

YOU

It’s a sequence, like a path. We need to step on the stones in the order the trees indicate.

The Tribe Leader observes, his expression thoughtful.

TRIBE LEADER

This brook tests our memory and observation. Tread carefully, like the path of faith.

[GAMEPLAY: Player must use the symbols on the trees as a guide to step on the correct stones in the brook and create a path across. A wrong step results in the stone sinking, and the player must restart the sequence.]

As you step on the stones in the correct sequence, they light up and solidify into firm steppingstones.

MIKA

We’re on the right path! Just a few more steps…

Once the sequence completes, a solid path stretches across the brook.

TRIBE LEADER

Our ancestors guide us. Forward, with faith and purpose.

FAST FORWARD TO THE END:

EXT. The Summit of Serenity - DAY

The Summit of Serenity stretches out beneath you. A profound silence envelops the peak.

You take a deep breath, and see the unity of the mountain, the earth, and every distant horizon. The wind whispers tales from far-off places, and sunlight bathes the summit in a golden hue.

Footsteps echo. Your tribe gathers around, their faces radiant with the glow of accomplishment. Mika steps closer, her eyes gleaming with a mix of admiration and curiosity.

MIKA

Such a sight, isn’t it?

The Tribe Leader gestures to the horizon. As the clouds part, a luminous temple emerges, standing tall and majestic, a beacon of knowledge and unity.

YOU

Looks amazing, but how do we get in?

[GAMEPLAY: Player must arrange tribe members in a specific pattern, matching symbols on their attire to those on the ground, to unlock the temple’s entrance.]

You, with a determined look, begin to decipher the riddle. Your eyes dart between the symbols on the ground and those worn by the tribe.

MIKA

The symbols… they might be the key.

The Tribe Leader nods, his gaze fixed on the temple.

TRIBE LEADER

Together, we unlock its doors.

As the sun dips below the horizon, the temple’s entrance glows. The last rays of the day cast long shadows on the temple’s threshold.

You and your tribe stand at the entrance. With a shared nod, you all step in.

INT. Summit Temple - DUSK

Inside, the temple is massive. The dimming light from outside filters through the temple’s windows and illuminates the ancient walls. Carvings of artisans, each engrossed in their craft, decorate the stone and tell tales of dedication and mastery.

In the temple’s heart, golden scrolls rest on a grand pedestal. Each artisan walks towards them.

The artisans unfurl the scrolls, but they reveal no ancient texts or guiding mantras. Instead, they mirror the very essence of the one holding them. Your reflection stares back.

Mika peers into her scroll and has a look of knowing.

MIKA

It’s us… our journey. The wisdom we sought is within.

The Tribe Leader, holding his scroll, nods in agreement.

TRIBE LEADER

The temple doesn’t bestow wisdom. It reflects it. We carry the lessons within us.

[GAMEPLAY: Player must interact with their scroll, piecing together fragmented memories of their journey. As they align these memories, the scroll glows, signifying the wisdom gained.]

You touch your scroll and memories of your journey flood back. Each challenge faced, each bond formed, and every lesson learned comes alive.

Mika’s eyes meet yours and smiles.

MIKA

We’ve found it, haven’t we? The true essence of our journey.

The Tribe Leader’s gaze sweeps over the gathered artisans and speaks.

TRIBE LEADER

We came seeking wisdom from the temple. But it was with us all along. Our journey, our experiences, that is our true wisdom.

With this revelation, the temple’s atmosphere starts to swirl. A harmonious energy envelops everyone and seals the bond between the artisans. This marks the end of one journey and the beginning of countless others.
