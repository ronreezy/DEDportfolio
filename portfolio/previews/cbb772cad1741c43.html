“Takeoff: A Symphony in the Sky”

Treatment

Artistic Goals

Logline

A serene yet exhilarating journey from the grounded hustle and bustle of the airport to the sublime tranquility of the open skies, captured through the lens of aviation.

Narrative

The narrative begins at the bustling airport, focusing on the workings of the ground crew. The climax of the narrative occurs with an action shot of our plane taking off, symbolizing the leap from ground to sky. Finally, the narrative concludes with a shot of another plane taking off, representing the freedom and beauty of being airborne.

Visual Style and Theme

The visual style will be cinematic, making use of wide and medium shots to capture the atmosphere and detail of the airport and the planes. The theme revolves around the exhilaration and peace that comes with taking to the skies.

Technical Approach

Tools and Software

The Galaxy S20+ offers shooting in a 16:9 ratio.

CapCut will be used to create a 1080p24fps video.

Formats and Specs

The Galaxy S20+ offers shooting in a 16:9 ratio and automatic adjustment of Shutter Speed, FPS, ISO, and Focus. This allows the user to focus on capturing the moments without worrying about adjusting the settings.

Management Plan

Time

I plan to spend 1 week on this project: a few days for pre-production and capturing footage, and the rest for post-production.

This project will be handled individually.

No additional materials are required beyond the smartphone, the editing software, and the chosen music track.

Production Plan

What process you will plan on taking to complete this project?

Selection of the music track, planning the shots to fit the pace and mood of the music, and rehearsing the camera movements.

Shooting at the airport, capturing a variety of angles and shots.

Editing the footage in CapCut to match the pacing and tempo of the music and exporting the final video as a H.264 file.
